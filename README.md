# Two-Sentence Horror

A horror story in exactly two sentences (214 characters):

> The new doorbell camera sends me a photo every time someone stands on the porch, and at 03:12 it sent one of me, standing on my own porch, waving. I have not left my bed all night, and the camera just sent another.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes that story to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic: the same text on every run.
